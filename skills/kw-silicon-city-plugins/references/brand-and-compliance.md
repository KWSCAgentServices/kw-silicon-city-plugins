# KW Silicon City branding and publication checks

Use this reference before creating or approving any artifact.

## Required brand asset

Use `../assets/kwsc-realtrends-logo-color.png`, the user-supplied KW Silicon City / Keller Williams / RealTrends Verified Best Brokerage logo.

The logo is mandatory on every independently usable marketing piece. For a multi-page PDF, deck, carousel, story set, or image bundle, place it on every page, slide, card, or image that may circulate on its own.

## Logo treatment

- Use the complete supplied logo; do not redraw, crop, recolor, rotate, stretch, rearrange, or cover it.
- Scale proportionally and keep it readable at the final delivery size.
- Logo background rule: preserve existing transparency. When the user requests background removal, remove only the surrounding background, not any part of the complete logo artwork, lettering or embedded RealTrends badge. The resulting asset must retain genuine transparency or a plain white background; never fill transparent areas with black.
- In a finished marketing design, place the complete logo on a plain white backing area, including within a dark layout. Do not place it directly on black or another dark background. Keep logo colors and all artwork unchanged.
- For a standalone background-removed logo export, use an alpha-capable format such as PNG to preserve transparency. If the destination cannot preserve alpha, flatten onto white, never black. Do not confuse a viewer's dark transparency preview with an actual black background; inspect alpha and the final intended placement before approving.
- Keep comfortable clear space around it and away from trim, fold, crop, and platform-safe-area boundaries.
- Do not place agent portraits, listing photos, QR codes, or text over the logo.
- If the logo cannot be legible in the requested size, simplify the layout or increase the canvas rather than omitting it.

## Content safeguards

- Keep the KWSC host/promoter's identity and listing-agent credit separate. For another agent's listing, include the verified "Listed by" line and listing brokerage when available on every independently usable piece and accompanying copy; label the KWSC block "Hosted by" or "Presented by" as appropriate.
- Apply the official KW palette and accessible approved template guidance in [kw-design-and-image-generation.md](kw-design-and-image-generation.md). The user-supplied complete logo remains mandatory.

- Market the property accurately. Do not invent amenities, views, renovations, school assignments, distances, awards, urgency, or comparative claims.
- Use property-first, value-first copy. Begin every standalone marketing piece with a short, verified attention headline, followed by no more than one brief hook, 3–5 scannable bullets, and a calm, low-pressure call to action. Do not deliver a dense paragraph, a one-sentence data dump, or an agent-led opening.
- Keep the tone useful and buyer-centered rather than salesy. Avoid unsupported urgency and clichés such as “dream home,” “won’t last,” “act fast,” or “once-in-a-lifetime.”
- In visual marketing, make verified property photography the dominant visual. Use one strong hero image plus useful supporting property photos when available. Keep the agent portrait and contact block smaller and clearly secondary while still readable.
- Label every numeric area stated in square feet with "Approx." directly beside that individual value—for example, "Approx. 1,850 sq. ft." Apply this to living area, lot size, rooms, garages, ADUs, patios, parcels, and any other square-foot measurement. A general disclaimer elsewhere does not satisfy this requirement.
- Avoid steering or language expressing a preference for protected classes. Describe the property and objective location features, not the presumed residents.
- Do not make unsupported legal, tax, investment-return, zoning, permit, school-quality, safety, or neighborhood-demographic claims.
- Retain required attribution or disclosure text visible on the source when applicable. Do not create a REALTOR®, MLS, Equal Housing, team, or agent logo from memory.
- Use the user's supplied headshot and branding only when authorized. Do not fabricate an agent portrait.
- If the original agent headshot cannot be confidently found, accessed, and visually verified, stop before visual generation and ask the user for the correct image or explicit authorization to proceed without a portrait. Never substitute initials, a monogram, silhouette, generic avatar, stock portrait, generated face, or other placeholder.
- Mandatory: provide the verified original Drive headshot directly to GPT Image with the full design source packet and require strong identity and appearance fidelity. Do not request or accept face generation, replacement, beautification, relighting, feature changes, an initials badge, or another substitute. Follow the complete-generation procedure in [kw-design-and-image-generation.md](kw-design-and-image-generation.md).
- The supplied agent headshot Drive folder is authorized for this workflow. Include the confidently matched agent's headshot and associated branding in agent-branded collateral while retaining the complete KW Silicon City logo. Scale portraits proportionally; use a clean crop without altering identity or appearance.
- Use only visually verified original photos from the supplied listing. Send those photos directly to GPT Image with the complete source packet and require the finished design to incorporate the actual supplied property rather than synthesize a house from text. Cropping and layout integration are acceptable; a different house, reconstruction, virtual staging, stock substitute, other-listing image, or artist's illustration is not. If no correct source photo is available, stop and ask the user.

## Visual release check

Inspect the rendered output at its final size. Confirm:

- the full logo is visible and legible on every piece, with unchanged artwork on white; standalone background-removed logo assets retain transparency or white, never an added black background. Reject a black-filled export or black/dark placement and correct it before release;
- host/promoter labeling and "Listed by" attribution are accurate and legible on every relevant piece and accompanying copy;
- property and agent photos are not swapped, distorted or misleadingly clipped; the agent's face is unchanged, with only proportional scaling and a clean crop allowed. Apply the side-by-side original-face release gate in the design reference. Recognizability alone and approval of the layout do not pass this check;
- the complete piece was generated and, if necessary, corrected through GPT Image only; no HTML, CSS, SVG, canvas, scripted layout, or code-based compositing was used to rebuild or patch the design;
- every displayed property is visibly grounded in the verified listing-photo inputs supplied to GPT Image; no different house, reconstructed scene, stock substitute, other-listing image, virtual staging, or artist's illustration appears;
- every agent-branded visual contains the confidently matched original headshot, unless the user explicitly authorized a no-portrait draft after being told the headshot was unavailable; no initials badge or other portrait substitute appears;
- key facts and disclaimers remain readable;
- the piece opens with a concise, verified attention headline; the body is brief and uses 3–5 scannable bullets rather than a dense paragraph or data dump;
- verified property photography is the visual hero, with multiple useful property photos when available; the agent portrait/contact block is smaller and secondary;
- the message leads with client value and uses a calm, non-pushy call to action;
- phone, email, URLs, QR codes, price, MLS number, status, and dates match the source record;
- every square-foot measurement is source-accurate and visibly includes "Approx." in the same text element; no unqualified square-foot value remains in the visual, caption, or accompanying copy;
- no sensitive or MLS-private fields appear;
- the artifact contains no placeholder text or unverified claims.
