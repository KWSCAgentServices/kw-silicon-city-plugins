# Industry output standards

Use channel-specific checks, not a generic claim of "industry compliant." Verify current official rules for the jurisdiction and publication channel when the output depends on them. Guidance reviewed 2026-10-13; this is operational support, not legal advice or a guarantee against lawsuits.

## California public advertising

- Check the person's actual role, current license status, and responsible brokerage. An internal roster match alone is not license verification.
- For materials within first-point-of-contact requirements, include the licensee's eight-digit DRE number and responsible broker's licensed identification. A branding logo alone does not prove that the broker-identification requirement is met. Do not assume the DBA/legal entity mapping.
- Do not shrink the DRE number below the smallest other text on covered solicitation materials. Keep the agent block secondary through layout, not by making required identifiers illegible. The phone should still be slightly larger than surrounding contact text.
- Only use REALTOR marks or membership descriptions with verified entitlement. Listing attribution and permission to advertise/use photography are separate checks.
- Verify facts, dates, source freshness, housing-neutral wording, and each Approx. area label. Never promise results, investment returns, or that a disclaimer removes legal exposure.

Primary references: [California DRE RE559](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re559.pdf), [DRE advertising guidelines](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re27.pdf), [DRE license lookup](https://www.dre.ca.gov/), and, where REALTOR membership applies, [NAR 2026 Code of Ethics, Article 12](https://www.nar.realtor/about-nar/governing-documents/code-of-ethics/2026-code-of-ethics-standards-of-practice).

## Output channel

- Social posts, announcements, flyers and promotional email: strong verified property/event headline, short benefit-led bullets, low-pressure CTA, exact attribution and readable required identifiers. "Just Listed" needs current listing-history evidence.
- MLS description: respect the destination's actual character/format limits and rules; use concise property-led prose when bullets or contact promotion are prohibited. Do not paste a social caption into restricted MLS fields.
- Private buyer/seller communication: answer the client's concern directly; use brief practical next steps, not a sales headline. Do not disclose unrelated client records.
- Comparative/market report: show source date, geographic boundary, period, sample count, sold versus active status, and limitations. Distinguish asking prices from closed prices. Show formulas and assumptions; do not present a comp table as a certified appraisal or precise market value.
- Offer/contract summary: ground each material term in the supplied document and page/section; distinguish executed, proposed, missing, and conflicting terms. Never infer a deadline from a generic escrow duration. Compute dates only from verified triggers, applicable language, and a known calendar; flag uncertain counting rules for professional review. Preserve exact contractual measurements in quotes; use Approx. for promotional adaptations, not alteration of the contract.
- Transaction checklist: separate verified contractual deadlines from suggested workflow dates, identify owner/status/dependency, and preserve uncertainty. A checklist does not itself create calendar events or contact other parties.
- Lead organization: use supplied facts and consent/preference signals, no demographic or protected-class scoring; recommend next steps without changing a CRM or sending messages unless requested.

## Readiness gate

Before handoff check identity/role, facts and freshness, attribution, privacy, channel format, measurements, required disclosures, and requested deliverable completeness. For visuals additionally compare property, face, logo and text against the original source packet. Fix recoverable defects through the authorized workflow. If a material gate remains unresolved, label the output accurately as a draft or stop the affected generation; never claim ready to publish or end-to-end tested.
