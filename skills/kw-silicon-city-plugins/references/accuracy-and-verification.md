# Accuracy and verification

Apply this reference to every KWSC task, including replies, research, copy, visuals, calculations and completion reports. Factual accuracy takes priority over a catchy hook, speed or a request for a ready-to-use outcome.

## Evidence before claims

Use the source that owns the fact: the current authorized roster for business contacts; official licensing records for license status; the primary listing for property details and attribution; an organizer's confirmation for event details; the supplied document and amendments for contract terms. Use current official sources for legal, lending, tax or regulatory information. Respect access limits. A search snippet, model memory, previous generated flyer or repeated syndication is not adequate confirmation of a publication-critical fact.

Keep a minimal internal claim record: value, exact source location, retrieval date, source update/data period when available, and whether it is source-reported, independently checked, user-confirmed or calculated. A fresh retrieval does not make old source data current. Verify changeable facts at the time of use. Follow the existing second-source procedure for stale or conflicting listing data. Do not expose private roster fields in evidence notes.

Never infer features, permissions, current availability, credentials, relationships or market outcomes from appearance or plausibility. A photographed room is not proof of permits or square footage. A lot size is not proof of ADU or subdivision rights. A roster match is not license verification. Public access to a photo is not reuse authorization. Attribute listing claims appropriately; do not imply inspection or independent measurement.

## Unknowns and conflicts

Retrieve the available authorized evidence yourself first. Do not ask the user to perform checks the tools can complete. When sources conflict, compare identity, scope and timestamps and seek the responsible primary source. Do not average conflicting values or choose the convenient one.

A user's factual correction is evidence to assess, not permission to falsify a record. Establish its source or direct authority; record any unresolved discrepancy. Never describe user-confirmed details as independently verified. A style preference can be applied without factual research.

If an optional fact cannot be confirmed, omit it and state a material omission briefly outside finished copy. If it affects identity, required attribution, permission, price/status, an advertised event or another essential claim, stop the affected deliverable and ask one consolidated question after completing independent checks. Do not call a blocked artifact ready to publish. Never substitute a plausible value, image or contact. State exactly what is unconfirmed, without claiming no source exists.

## Calculations and analysis

Use sourced inputs, check arithmetic and units, and retain enough precision to reproduce the result. Show the formula or method when it affects a decision. Keep living area separate from lot area and asking prices separate from closed prices.

Provide estimates, projections or scenarios only when the task calls for them and the evidence supports the method. Label them clearly, disclose inputs and assumptions, and distinguish interpretation from measured facts. Do not manufacture missing inputs or give a guessed valuation. If the evidence cannot support a conclusion, say so. Approx. is a measurement qualifier, not permission to invent an area.

## Final and completion checks

Compare every displayed factual claim with the approved source record, including headline implications, small print, image text and campaign adaptations. Remove unsupported claims and recheck corrected outputs. Keep status-specific price/date omissions, truthful roles, source-photo fidelity, privacy and required disclosures unchanged.

Use "verified", "tested", "uploaded", "updated", "published" and "complete" only for actions actually performed and supported by results. A package validator checks structure; roster tests check roster behavior. Neither proves live marketing accuracy or end-to-end host behavior. Report the checks run and remaining limits without claiming universal accuracy.

For plugin and repository changes, confirm the save/push result and read back the changed source before reporting verification. A private saved release is not a public listing or a confirmed update to every user's installed copy. If read-back or an end-to-end check is unavailable, distinguish the saved change from the unverified behavior.
