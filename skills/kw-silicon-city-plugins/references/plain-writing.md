# Plain writing

Apply this preference to KWSC copy, captions, emails, texts, reports and replies.

Editorial reference: [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), read October 15, 2026. Its observations are context-dependent clues, not authorship tests or universal prohibitions. Use the guidance to improve substance and readability; never promise detector results or disguise authorship.

## Editing rules

- State specific facts. Cut inflated importance, generic praise and unsupported interpretation.
- Name actual sources and relationships. Avoid vague authorities or invented consensus.
- Use everyday words and direct verbs, including is, are and has. Remove stock buzzwords.
- Cut rhetorical contrasts, forced triples, repetitive conclusions and generic future outlooks.
- Keep terminology consistent. Vary sentence length naturally without artificial synonym swapping.
- Use sentence case, restrained formatting and ordinary punctuation. Avoid decorative emoji, excessive bold and unnecessary tables or headings.
- Keep chatbot commentary, placeholders and editing instructions out of finished copy.
- Check citations against their claims. Remove broken links, fabricated references and leaked tool markup.

## KWSC application

Keep the user's event sequence, useful fact bullets, full address, clear date/time and relevant CTA. Use plain hooks without decorative stickers under the October 15 writing preference. Genuine campaign headings, required branding, hashtags, listing attribution and app rendering syntax remain appropriate to their destination.

Make the benefit specific to the supplied event or property. State who should attend, including Clients & their Agents when applicable. Each campaign message needs its own useful detail; changing adjectives does not create a new angle. Use a registration deadline only when confirmed. Use Sold Out only when verified and pair it with an appropriate waitlist or next-event action.

Before delivery, read the text aloud mentally. Rewrite any line that could advertise almost any event or home. Confirm the reader can find the useful facts and next step. Report a material unknown briefly outside the finished copy; never fill it with a guess. Preserve exact quotations and required legal wording.

## Additional drafting checks

Skip canned greetings, automatic offers for more work and repeated closing summaries. Keep assistant commentary outside finished copy. In newly drafted messages, use ordinary punctuation without em dashes; keep quote marks consistent. Exact quotations and required legal wording remain unchanged.

State the actual role when mentioning an agent or brokerage. If a lookup fails, name the missing verification only when it affects the task; do not claim that information does not exist or speculate about personal lives. Never invent testimonials, personal experiences or sources.

Check the final destination for raw citation IDs, broken formatting and unresolved placeholders. Use a real template only when requested. Do not add AI-provider tracking tags to shared links; preserve working signed URLs and authorized campaign parameters.

Keep Approx. with each square-foot value. Omit prices for In Contract and dates from Just Listed/Just Closed unless requested, as already defined in the status rules. Event dates remain visible when the event requires them. Privacy and source-verification gates still apply. Automated package and roster tests do not measure writing quality.
